# Taste log

Every decision Karen and Kristin make about the look, feel and content, with the reason when they gave one. Newest at the top. Claude adds an entry whenever new feedback arrives, then updates [`brand-brain.md`](brand-brain.md) if the decision changes the bigger picture.

Format: `date · area · decision · why (if said)`

## 2026-09-30 · Round 3 (the chosen design)

- About · The lace collage runs behind the menu from the very top edge; the menu stays in the same spot as on every other page · consistency across pages, lace still dominant.
- Cursor · **Standard browser cursor. Do not change it.** An inverted white cursor ("white wedding dress") was tested on one page and rejected, as were the pink shard and pearl pin earlier.
- Contact · Date line "Copenhagen, 2026" in Helvetica ultra light; the rest of the letter stays typewriter.
- About · Typewriter "Ode to Shelly" scrap removed from the page.
- About · "Upcyclnig" was a typo to fix (unlike "Silhoutte" on Home, which stays).
- About · Total makeover from their own mockup (`docs/mockups/about-page-mockup.png`): layered lace strips across the top, no "Two GIRLS" headline, intro right, small defect lace left. Bottom text area kept but spread out with more space.
- Contact · Was too similar to Home and Collection; now a typewritten letter (liked).
- Collection · Tulle and ribbon (waistband) photos removed; all pieces show `[fig. missing]`.
- Home · "Done for now." The added coordinates and blinking `>>_` were not liked: reverted to the v1 home page.
- Base · **Torn Page v1 is the base** of the site; they liked the first version best.
- Process · When a version is chosen as the base, build on it as it is. Do not carry over things tried in other rounds unless asked.
