The story of Shelly

Shelly was last seen in the spring of 1974, leaving a dance in Hellerup in somebody else’s veil. Some say she was a seamstress, some say a lighthouse, some say a very small comet. Blahblahblah, blimblamblum: she still sends a postcard every June, unsigned.

Our approach to making

Upcycling, rethinking and repurposing: we upcycle local materials and previously loved garments and revive carefully selected stagnant fabrics, combining them with craftsmanship. Each garment is a testimony of individuality. Borrowing from couture in how we design and make, we blur the boundaries between couture and ready-to-wear.
